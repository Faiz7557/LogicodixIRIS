# Pengguna lapangan karhutla Indonesia: segmen, alur kerja, pain point

Reading notes: **[S]** = taken from a search-result summary only, full page not opened, verify before citing in the final pitch. Everything else was read on the page or in the regulation text. Years in brackets are the year of the source. The 2026 El Nino season is the freshest evidence; 2016-2018 items are outside the preferred window and are kept only where nothing newer exists. ## Q1. Manggala Agni Daops: size, coverage, hotspot intake, patrol types, reporting tools

### Takeaway
About 34 Daops with 1,500-1,800 people cover 12 provinces, and they work a simple loop of SiPongi hotspot, groundcheck, early suppression, education. The official system for patrol data (SIPP Karhutla) exists, but no source found describes how a regu actually logs a groundcheck day to day.

### Cited Findings
- 34 Daops in 2020 across 12 provinces (Sumut, Riau, Kepri, Jambi, Sumsel, Kalbar, Kalteng, Kalsel, Kaltim, Sulsel, Sultra, Sulut); more than 1,800 members in 2019 — [Wikipedia ID](https://id.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manggala_Agni). A roughly 2022 report says more than 1,500 personnel in 34 Daops, so the headcount conflicts — [Media Indonesia](https://mediaindonesia.com/humaniora/522313/klhk-ingin-personel-manggala-agni-masuk-jabatan-fungsional) [S].
- Work areas cross provincial lines and long travel to fires prompted plans for new Daops — [Wikipedia ID](https://id.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manggala_Agni). Aceh had no Daops at the time of one report — [AJNN](https://www.ajnn.net/news/aceh-butuh-manggala-agni-untuk-tangani-kasus-karhutla/index.html) [S]; a Nagan Raya Daops was being set up as of 2023 — [Wikipedia ID](https://id.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manggala_Agni).
- Single-Daops example: Daops Sumatera VI/Siak has 60 personnel (2026) — [Tribun Pekanbaru](https://pekanbaru.tribunnews.com/riau-region/1112136/manggala-agni-perketat-patroli-di-sejumlah-wilayah-siak-sisir-perkampungan-rawan-karhutla) [S].
- Workflow at Daops Kalimantan IX/Singkawang (2026): monitor via SiPongi plus satellite data; verify every hotspot on the ground (218 hotspots Feb-Jul); if confirmed, extinguish, cool, and educate residents. Routine and independent (mandiri) patrols target high-risk areas; integrated patrols use a joint team. The article does not say how many hotspots were real fires — [Antara Kalbar, 28 Jul 2026](https://kalbar.antaranews.com/berita/707797/manggala-agni-deteksi-218-hotspot-di-singkawang-sejak-februari-2026).
- Older description (2017): no-hotspot areas get weather monitoring and outreach; hotspot areas get groundcheck and early suppression. Integrated patrols include TNI, Polri, village officials and MPA; 300 village posts in 8 provinces reached 1,204 fire-prone villages — [JPNN, Okt 2017](https://m.jpnn.com/news/kerja-keras-manggala-agni-menurunkan-titik-api).
- SiPongi takes NASA Terra/Aqua, SNPP and NOAA-20 data; the ministry reports high-confidence hotspots and repeats that one fire can show as several hotspots — [Kemenhut, SP.628, 20 Okt 2026](https://www.kehutanan.go.id/news/hotspot-turun-617-titik-operasi-karhutla-tetap-diperkuat). Point-level confidence is public — [Kemenhut SiPongi+](https://www.kehutanan.go.id/news/cek-langsung-si-pongi-akses-informasi-karhutla-terbaru-dan-transparan) [S].
- SIPP Karhutla (Sistem Informasi Patroli Pencegahan) has been used by Manggala Agni in Sumatra since 2021, with a Dirjen PPI regulation as legal basis — [Syaufina et al., IPB policy brief, 2022](https://journal.ipb.ac.id/agro-maritim/article/view/53687) (abstract only).
- SOPs sit in Perdirjen PPI P.12/2020 and the functional post in Permen PANRB 54/2022 — [Wikipedia ID](https://id.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manggala_Agni). Parent rule: [Permen LHK P.32/2016](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/298069) [S].
- After peak season, the ministry says falling hotspot numbers do not end operations because conditions still need patrol and groundcheck — [goindonesia.id, 6 Nov 2026](https://www.goindonesia.id/hotspot-nasional-turun-tajam-tinggal-55-titik-operasi-lapangan-terus-dilanjutkan/) [S].

### Inferences
- 1,500-1,800 people over 34 Daops is about 44-53 people per Daops, consistent with the 60 at Siak. Any tool for this segment has to save a small team travel time, not add reporting.
- SIPP already exists for Sumatra. A hackathon build that re-creates patrol logging for Manggala Agni would duplicate a mandated government system. The gap is more likely below Daops level (MPA, village, BPBD kabupaten).

### Gaps
- Area per regu: no source found. Regu size: not confirmed in any source opened (a 15-person figure could not be verified).
- WhatsApp or paper forms for groundcheck reporting: no source found either way. This needs a phone call to one Daops.
- Share of hotspots that turn out to be real fires at groundcheck: not published in anything found.

## Q2. MPA and Desa Mandiri Peduli Api: membership, money, equipment, sustainability, connectivity

### Takeaway
MPA numbers are large on paper (more than 11,000 in 2023), but evidence from 2017 to 2026 keeps showing the same weakness: no recurring money for fuel, hose, boats or well maintenance. When village budgets shrink, operations stop.

### Cited Findings
- More than 11,000 community firefighters nationally; field workers say early under-investment and poor coordination weakened the network. Bathin Sobanga, Riau: 5 volunteers led by Indra My Lubis, drone bought from village funds about 2020. Article gives no pay data — [Mongabay via Eco-Business, Jul 2023](https://www.eco-business.com/news/as-el-nino-brings-wildfires-indonesias-volunteer-firefighters-gear-up).
- Desa Kalibandung, Kubu Raya, Kalbar (2026): no 2026 fire budget; village funds fell from about Rp1 miliar to about Rp300 juta under efficiency cuts, and MPA operations suffered. Residents pooled money for fuel; partner NGOs gave equipment and operating money. Residents had only 100 m of hose and had to join lengths. Suppression had run 25 days by publication; a 30 ha plot was lost (about Rp60 juta) — [Mongabay, 31 Agu 2026](https://mongabay.co.id/2026/08/31/kebakaran-gambut-kalbar-tak-kunjung-padam/).
- Walhi Kalbar's Indra Syahnanda says limited budgets hit equipment quality, incentives and operating costs for firefighters; BPBD Kalbar's Daniel says the province holds fewer than 10 peat-injection tools — [Mongabay, 31 Agu 2026](https://mongabay.co.id/2026/08/31/kebakaran-gambut-kalbar-tak-kunjung-padam/).
- Pulang Pisau, Kalteng (2021): at Talio Hulu only 8 of 50 wells still work. Kantan Atas MPA head Stevanus Purwudi says many wells are unusable for lack of an operating budget; 10 pumps were still held by prosecutors as case evidence; Talio Muara has one pump. Bahaur Basantan MPA works with 12 neighbouring villages — [Mongabay, 6 Okt 2021](https://mongabay.co.id/2021/10/06/melihat-kesiapan-desa-di-wilayah-gambut-kalimantan-tengah-hadapi-karhutla/).
- OKI, Sumsel (2017): MPA chairs Fasirin (Riding) and Edi Saputra (Perigi) report no operational funds even for pre-fire patrols; patrols need rented boats, which Fasirin compares in cost to a one-way flight to Jakarta. Riding received 1 pump, 1 backpack pump, 1 GPS, uniforms for 5. Desa Bukit Batu had no MPA at all — [Mongabay, 31 Agu 2017](https://mongabay.co.id/2017/08/31/menanti-peran-optimal-masyarakat-peduli-api-di-bentang-alam-padang-sugihan/).
- Six-village study in Riau and Kalteng found lack of money for operating and maintaining rewetting infrastructure among the most common problems, and recommends prioritising MPA funding — [Community-Based Fire Management and Peatland Restoration in Indonesia, 2023](https://www.aminer.org/pub/648d58aad68f896eface9a32/community-based-fire-management-and-peatland-restoration-in-indonesia) [S]. Authors not confirmed; the page returned no text.
- Jambi MPA members paid only from a jointly managed plot (2017) — [Mongabay](https://www.mongabay.co.id/2017/01/15/cerita-para-penjaga-lahan-gambut-dari-jambi/) [S].
- Local studies list finance, motivation and facilities (Kesuma, Riau, 2021) and lack of government or private support (Siak, 2018) as obstacles — [JIL UNRI](https://jil.ejournal.unri.ac.id/index.php/JIL/article/view/2282/Creative%20Commons%20Attribution%204.0%20International%20License.) [S]; [Jurnal Antropologi, 2018](https://doaj.org/article/8acc8bd0d9824924bb77187d88f21292) [S].
- Personal risk: Ahmad Riyadi, head of kelurahan fire volunteers, died 27 Aug 2026 after fighting peat fires; cause not confirmed — [Mongabay, 3 Sep 2026](https://mongabay.co.id/2026/09/03/nestapa-warga-kalimantan-hirup-racun-asap-kebakaran-hutan-dan-gambut/).
- Policy direction: a new peat agency is reported to plan a village base reviving the MPA role — [Kompas.id](https://www.kompas.id/artikel/hampir-dua-tahun-setelah-brgm-berakhir-pemerintah-bentuk-badan-gambut-baru) [S].
- Connectivity, national: about 2,000 villages still blank-spot, majority in six Papua provinces — [Detik](https://inet.detik.com/telecommunication/d-8228598/dirut-bakti-komdigi-2-000-desa-blankspot-segera-dialiri-internet) [S]; [Investortrust](https://investortrust.id/national/112616/sebanyak-2000-desa-di-indonesia-masih-blankspot-mayoritas-di-papua) [S].
- Connectivity, Kalimantan: 1,162 Kalteng villages and kelurahan had 4G at end 2025, up 71 — [Databoks/BPS](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/layanan-konsumen-kesehatan/statistik/745a93ca8da6289/jumlah-desa-dan-kelurahan-tercover-sinyal-4g-lte-jaringan-di-kalimantan-tengah-2025). Opensignal 2025: some Kalimantan, Maluku and Papua districts spend more than 5% of measured time with no signal against under 1% in the west — [TechTimes citing Opensignal, 2026](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/326456/20260903/indonesia-pollution-index-hits-1023-worst-kalimantan-haze-sends-millions-students-online.htm).

### Inferences
- The recurring problem is the budget for consumables and maintenance, more than training or awareness. A tool that surfaces "which of our 50 wells work, what we lack, what it costs" feeds a real budget decision.
- Village-level signal statistics overstate field connectivity, because a village counted as covered can still have no signal deep in peat reachable only by boat. Design offline-first regardless.

### Gaps
- Honorarium amounts: no figure found. DMPA (Desa Mandiri Peduli Api) programme evaluations: not found.
- A count of inactive MPAs: none found. Evidence of inactivity is case-based only.
- Fire-prone village list crossed with Podes or BAKTI signal data: not found; Podes 2024 province values are paywalled or not surfaced.

## Q3. Kepala desa and BPBD kabupaten: siaga, triggers, Dana Desa rules

### Takeaway
Dana Desa 2026 explicitly allows fire prevention and no-burn land clearing, so the legal door is open. The practical failure is prioritisation: after several quiet years, villages spend elsewhere, then have nothing when fire arrives.

### Cited Findings
- Permendes PDT 16/2025 (Dana Desa 2026), section B on climate-resilient and disaster-resilient villages, lists no-burn land clearing via Padat Karya Tunai Desa, and forest and land fire prevention and response such as building or maintaining sumur bor and water channels on peat — [Permendes PDT 16/2025](https://www.peraturan.go.id/filespengundangan/permendespdt-no-16-tahun-2025.pdf) (text extracted and checked).
- Permendes 7/2023 lists disaster-risk maps and light fire extinguishers among fundable items — [Permendesa 7/2023](https://www.peraturan.go.id/files/permendesa-no-7-tahun-2023.pdf) [S]. Permendes 8/2022 lists disaster mitigation as a priority for 2023 — [Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/dana-desa-lt61ce6eac3b619?page=3) [S].
- Kalibandung's village secretary Syahroni explains funds went to other programmes after six fire-free years: "Kami pikir tidak akan terjadi kebakaran tahun ini" — [Mongabay, 31 Agu 2026](https://mongabay.co.id/2026/08/31/kebakaran-gambut-kalbar-tak-kunjung-padam/).
- Status trigger example: Pemkab Gunung Mas with Forkopimda set Siaga II for 1 Aug-31 Oct 2026, based on BPBD's July hotspot trend and BMKG's rainfall forecast. Camat, kepala desa and damang were told to step up education and prevention; MPA to be trained; BPBD opens a main post plus 3 sub-posts with mixed patrols and drones — [InfoPublik, Jul 2026](https://infopublik.id/kategori/nusantara/979756/hotspot-meningkat-pemkab-gunung-mas-berlakukan-siaga-karhutla-hingga-akhir-oktober).
- Eight Kalsel areas were on emergency alert by Sep 2026; critics say officials rely on local-government reports rather than direct engagement with communities — [Mongabay, 12 Okt 2026](https://mongabay.co.id/2026/10/12/kebakaran-hutan-perlu-langkah-serius-bukan-sekadar-kedatangan-pejabat/).
- Village heads' association proposes "Desa Zero Karhutla" with rewards for fire-free villages (Sep 2026) — [Metro TV](https://www.metrotvnews.com/read/NOlC9Xro-asosiasi-kepala-desa-dorong-pencegahan-karhutla-berbasis-desa) [S]. Counter-evidence: a West Kalimantan RCT in 75 treatment villages (2018) found no causal effect on fire from payments of Rp10 juta and a conditional Rp150 juta — [Devpolicy, 18 Sep 2026](https://devpolicy.org/rethinking-kalimantans-recurring-fires-a-super-wicked-problem-20260918/).
- Hulu Sungai Selatan village task forces are said to use Dana Desa for operations (2023) — [BPK Kalsel clipping](https://kalsel.bpk.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Dana-Desa-Tim-Satgas-Kabupaten-HSS-Pantau-Wilayah-Rawan-24-Jam-Operasional-Pakai-Dana-Desa.pdf) [S].

### Inferences
- A village budget aid that turns a risk history and a well or pump inventory into a ready Musrenbangdes line item, citing Permendes 16/2025 section B, targets a specific moment and person (sekdes or kades during APBDes planning).
- Reward schemes are a weak basis for an MVP given the RCT null result.

### Gaps
- Standard national triggers for siaga vs tanggap darurat at kabupaten level: not found. Gunung Mas is one example only.
- Share of Dana Desa actually spent on fire prevention: no data found.

## Q4. Timing chain from ignition to first response; escalation on peat

### Takeaway
No measured ignition-to-response dataset for Indonesia was found. What exists is anecdotal, and points to hours before a report arrives and days of burning once access is poor.

### Cited Findings
- Palangkaraya, 11 Sep 2016 (roadside peat): fire about 17:00, report from a Babinsa about 19:30, Manggala Agni on site about 21:00, out about 22:00. Tasik Payawan subdistrict head Ciing said officers typically arrive about three hours after a report — [Mongabay, 2016](https://mongabay.co.id/2016/09/12/di-malam-takbir-area-gambut-ini-malah-terbakar/).
- Muara Ohong, Kutai Barat, Aug 2026: residents noticed fire on 13 Aug; still burning 14 Aug. Kepala Kampung Aliansyah says no land or water route exists, so residents can only watch; line estimated at several km — [Kompas, 14 Agu 2026](https://regional.kompas.com/read/2026/08/14/193507378/karhutla-di-kutai-barat-kaltim-meluas-apa-yang-menjadi-kendala?page=all).
- Water: Palangka Raya crews increasingly rely on residents' sumur bor far from the fire as canals shrink — [Kantamedia, 2026](https://www.kantamedia.com/kalimantan-tengah/palangka-raya/sumber-air-makin-langka-pemadaman-karhutla-di-gambut-kian-sulit/) [S]. Sembilang NP fires are far from water — [IDN Times](https://sumsel.idntimes.com/news/sumatra-selatan/medan-sulit-jadi-kendala-penanganan-karhutla-di-taman-nasional-sembilang-00-pbgds-3vhgb8) [S]. Kalsel teams dug new canals and wells to bring water closer — [Nusantara Post](https://www.nusantarapost.news/buffer-zones/pemadaman-karhutla-kalimantan-selatan-terkendala-akses-sumber-air) [S].
- Peat needs high pressure or wetting agents; roughly 2-6 litres of water per kg burned — [Liputan6, 2026](https://www.liputan6.com/news/read/8311863/berapa-banyak-air-dan-waktu-untuk-padamkan-karhutla-kalimantan-dan-sumsel) [S].
- Peat fire persists below ground, flames can drop while smoke continues, and detection is hard; BPBD Kalsel uses thermal drones and surfactant — [Mongabay, 12 Okt 2026](https://mongabay.co.id/2026/10/12/kebakaran-hutan-perlu-langkah-serius-bukan-sekadar-kedatangan-pejabat/).
- A vendor claims detection 35 minutes faster than NASA's public system and argues the decisive window is the first hours while fire is on the surface. This is a company claim, not an independent evaluation — [Eco-Business, 2026](https://www.eco-business.com/news/ai-wildfire-platform-seeks-indonesia-partnerships-as-regional-haze-worsens/) [S].
- Satellites can miss small fires and low-heat underground burning; dried peat can smoulder for weeks or months; water-bombing is often grounded by smoke — [Malay Mail, 1 Nov 2026](https://www.malaymail.com/amp/news/world/2026/11/01/indonesias-fire-hotspots-fall-sharply-but-underground-peat-blazes-persist/241580) [S].

### Inferences
- In the one fully timed case, the ignition-to-report gap (about 2.5 h) was longer than report-to-arrival (about 1.5 h). This is one 2016 roadside case and should not be generalised, but it suggests testing the "first report" step, not only dispatch.
- Satellite speed-ups do not help where crews cannot reach the fire. Route and water knowledge may matter more than earlier detection in such sites.

### Gaps
- No peer-reviewed or official measured response-time data found. No sourced figure for how many hours separate a controllable surface fire from an established peat fire. Tacconi, Medrilzam and Carmenta work on this point was not located in this pass.
- Suggest the team frame this as "nobody measures it" rather than quoting a number.

## Q5. Smallholders: why burn, cost, criminalisation, permit or notification mechanisms

### Takeaway
Burning is cheap and the alternatives are not financed. The legal position is split by province and changed abruptly in 2026, while the working notification mechanisms that exist are paper letters routed through the kepala desa.

### Cited Findings
**Cost**
- Kades of Anjir Kalampan, Kalteng, estimated no-burn clearing at about ten times burning, about Rp20 juta/ha (2018) — [Mongabay](https://mongabay.co.id/2018/05/25/yanir-berkali-gagal-hingga-berhasil-kembangkan-pertanian-tanpa-bakar-di-lahan-gambut/) [S].
- BRG quoted about Rp200 ribu/ha for decomposer solution alone (2017) — [Republika](https://ekonomi.republika.co.id/berita/ok891i368/brg-siapkan-tiga-solusi-cegah-pembakaran-lahan) [S]. Not comparable to the Rp20 juta full-cost estimate.
- A study cited by Mongabay puts fire at about US$200/ha against US$595/ha mechanical, varying by land and cost items — [Mongabay short article](https://mongabay.co.id/short-article/marak-karhutla-studi-tentang-murahnya-membuka-lahan-dengan-cara-dibakar-jadi-sorotan/) [S].
- High up-front cost is why farmers in Sagatani, Singkawang have not adopted no-burn — [Ziraa'ah](https://ojs.uniska-bjm.ac.id/index.php/ziraah/article/view/5862) [S].
- At Kantan Atas, Pulang Pisau, land-clearing bans pushed about 70% of farmland into oil palm and rubber (2021) — [Mongabay](https://mongabay.co.id/2021/10/06/melihat-kesiapan-desa-di-wilayah-gambut-kalimantan-tengah-hadapi-karhutla/).
- Not all burning is subsistence: in four Riau districts, burned land sold at about US$856/ha against US$665/ha cut-and-slash, with farmer-group organisers taking most of the revenue — [Mongabay on Purnomo et al., 2017](https://news.mongabay.com/2017/01/how-local-elites-earn-money-from-burning-land-in-indonesia/) [S]. Bareskrim in 2026 described suspects' motive as cheap clearing for oil palm; 72 suspects named — [Jakarta Post, 24 Agu 2026](https://www.thejakartapost.com/indonesia/2026/08/24/police-crack-down-on-land-burning-in-kalimantan).

**Criminalisation**
- Sona, 53, Talang Mamak woman, Desa Sanglap, Inhu, Riau: burned a plot to plant rice for her family; hotspot traced through a police monitoring app; detained from 27 Aug 2024; prosecutors sought 1 year and Rp500 juta although acknowledging non-commercial traditional farming — [Mongabay, 18 Mar 2025](https://mongabay.co.id/2025/03/18/mau-berladang-perempuan-talang-mamak-terjerat-kasus-karhutla/).
- Gusti Maulidin, 63, and Sarwani, Rungun, Kotawaringin Barat: tried over about 1 ha; AMAN questions why enforcement falls on small farmers (opinion piece by AMAN staff) — [Mongabay, Des 2019](https://mongabay.co.id/2019/12/10/menyasar-dan-memenjarakan-para-peladang/).
- Six Sintang peladang acquitted 9 Mar 2020 — [Mongabay](https://mongabay.co.id/2020/03/18/tidak-terbukti-bakar-lahan-enam-peladang-di-sintang-divonis-bebas/) [S]. Two Kapuas Hulu peladang got 5 months — [Antara Kalbar](https://kalbar.antaranews.com/berita/406226/dua-peladang-kapua-s-hulu-divonis-lima-bulan-penjara-terkait-karhutla) [S].
- Kalsel 2026: police said most individual suspects were farmers clearing by fire — [Mongabay, 12 Okt 2026](https://mongabay.co.id/2026/10/12/kebakaran-hutan-perlu-langkah-serius-bukan-sekadar-kedatangan-pejabat/).

**Rules and mechanisms**
- UU 32/2009 Pasal 69(2) lets traditional farmers burn up to 2 ha with notification and firebreaks; applying zero-burning to them is argued to contradict the law — [Devpolicy, Sep 2026](https://devpolicy.org/rethinking-kalimantans-recurring-fires-a-super-wicked-problem-20260918/).
- Perda Kalteng 1/2020 Pasal 5-6: burning banned, except non-peat land by Masyarakat Hukum Adat farmers; max 1 ha/KK for rice or seasonal food crops; max 20 ha per village per day; not simultaneous within 1 km; no exemption once the Governor declares Siaga Darurat; details left to a Pergub — [BPK Kalteng note, 2020](https://kalteng.bpk.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Akhirnya-Perda-Membersihkan-Lahan-Dengan-Cara-Dibakar-Disahkan.pdf). Indigenous groups wanted at least 2 ha — [JIRReG](https://jurnal.apmd.ac.id/index.php/JIRReG/article/download/177/115/572) [S].
- Perbup Sintang 2018 (number reads as 57 in a poor OCR): notification letter to the Lurah or Kades using a set form; max 2 ha/KK, local varieties only; kades must block burning beyond 10 ha per village per day, schedule burns to avoid that, and forward letters with a recap to BPBD via camat; no burning under drought conditions or once Bupati declares tanggap darurat — [FAOLEX PDF](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/ins213248.pdf) (OCR text checked; several clauses garbled).
- Perda Kalbar 1/2022 allowed up to 2 ha/KK with firebreaks, banned on peat. President ordered repeal on 22 Aug 2026; protest at DPRD Kalbar on 27 Aug warned of renewed criminalisation — [Mongabay, 31 Agu 2026](https://mongabay.co.id/2026/08/31/kebakaran-gambut-kalbar-tak-kunjung-padam/). No confirmation that repeal is complete; one source gives a different Perda number — [Metro TV](https://www.metrotvnews.com/read/kj2CDV4d-presiden-minta-perda-kalbar-1-2022-dicabut-ini-isinya) [S]; [Antara](https://kalbar.antaranews.com/berita/545391/kalbar-gunakan-pendekatan-kearifan-lokal-cegah-karhutla) [S].

### Inferences
- The Sintang mechanism is effectively a daily village quota managed on paper, and the Kalteng Perda has the same structure at 20 ha. A digital register for the kades is a sharply defined user and workflow. Its legal standing is the main risk because rules are shifting (Kalbar) and any tool could be read as encouraging burning.
- Burn eligibility depends on status changes (siaga darurat, tanggap darurat) a farmer may not hear about. That status lookup is low-risk and useful alone.
- "Petani" must be split: subsistence peladang versus land-speculation clearing, which have opposite incentives.

### Gaps
- The Kalteng Pergub implementing rules and any Sumsel Perda or Pergub on burn notification were not retrieved.
- Whether Sintang notification letters are actually used in practice: no field source found.
- Carmenta and Medrilzam smallholder findings were not retrieved; no independent per-hectare no-burn cost study for smallholders was opened.

## Q6. Citizens affected by haze: vulnerable groups, local air-quality info, schools, puskesmas, shelters

### Takeaway
Scale of harm is well documented for 2026, and school decisions now hinge on an ISPU number. But monitors are sparse, decisions can rest on one station, and clean-air shelters exist without a clear way to find them.

### Cited Findings
- Kemenkes as of 2 Nov 2026: 401,586 cumulative ISPA cases in 7 provinces and 63 kab/kota, including 91,913 under-5s, 87,704 aged 6-9, 56,092 over 60, 590 pregnant women, 1,530 asthma patients, 8,086 outdoor workers. On 3 Nov, 2 areas hazardous, 15 unhealthy — [InfoPublik, 4 Nov 2026](https://infopublik.id/kategori/bencana/995999/kemenkes-intensifkan-pemantauan-dampak-karhutla-di-tujuh-provinsi).

## Q9. Pain points ranked; real segments and MVP fit

### Takeaway
By frequency and severity, the top problems are money for local response, physical access to fire and water, and contested burn rules. The best software-sized openings are not the top-ranked physical shortages, but the information steps around them.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
Candidate problem statements, most defensible first:
1. A village MPA chair cannot show which wells and pumps work or what is missing before the dry season, so nothing enters the APBDes under Permendes 16/2025. Evidence: Q2, Q3. Lowest overlap with existing systems.
2. Parents of infants in haze cities find clean-air shelters by chance. Evidence: Q6. Quick to build; season-dependent.
3. A kades managing burn notices and a daily hectare cap has only paper and no live status. Evidence: Q5. Sharp workflow, but the legal climate is the risk.
4. A resident's fire report gets no acknowledgement. Evidence: Q7. Needs a BPBD partner to be real, and PetaBencana is adjacent.

Avoid: another hotspot map (SiPongi+ is public), a patrol log for Manggala Agni (SIPP exists), reward-for-no-fire apps (RCT null result in Q3).

### Gaps
- Nothing here is primary user research. One short call each with an MPA chair, a Daops officer, and a BPBD kabupaten operator would validate ranks 1, 2 and 6, and answer the WhatsApp and paper-form question.
