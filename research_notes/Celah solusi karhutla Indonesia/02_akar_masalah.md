# Akar masalah dan kegagalan tata kelola karhutla Indonesia

As of 9 Nov 2026. Tags: **[SNIPPET]** = seen only in search-result text, page not opened or blocked. **[WEAK]** = only one weak source (opinion, single anecdote, unattributed). Untagged = page was opened and read. Percentages marked "my calc" are arithmetic on cited figures.

## 1. Ignition causes and their shares

### Takeaway
No nationally representative split of ignitions by actor (smallholder / company / accident / arson / speculation) was found. What exists is "human-caused" totals, burned area inside concessions, and local actor-network studies; any single percentage split in a pitch would be unsupported.

### Cited Findings
- BNPB: 99% of the 328,724 ha burned in 2019 was human-caused. No breakdown by actor or by land clearing — [Mongabay 2019](https://www.mongabay.co.id/2019/11/16/kebakaran-hutan-dan-lahan-terus-terjadi-bagaimana-solusinya/)
- KLHK names three main factors: humans, damaged peat ecosystems, weather [SNIPPET] — [Republika 2019](https://news.republika.co.id/berita/py5wtk409/klhk-sebut-tiga-faktor-utama-penyebab-karhutla)
- Purnomo et al. 2017 (*Forest Policy and Economics*; 4 Riau districts, 10 sites, 131 respondents): farmer-group organisers (district elites) take the majority of revenue from burned land; slashers/burners and village elites who issue land documents also get a cut. Land trades at $856/ha burned vs $665/ha cut-and-slashed, and $3,800/ha burned and already planted with oil palm. The article gives no percentage split — [Mongabay summary](https://news.mongabay.com/2017/01/how-local-elites-earn-money-from-burning-land-in-indonesia/)
- Purnomo et al. 2019 (*International Forestry Review*): fire use strongly influenced by local politics, with fire activity rising about a year before local elections [SNIPPET; PDF returned 401] — [CIFOR-ICRAF](https://cifor-icraf.org/publications/pdf_files/articles/APurnomo1902.pdf)
- Greenpeace overlay 2015-2019: 3,650,885 ha burned (4,440,494 ha cumulative counting re-burns). Oil palm concessions 621,524 ha; HTI 679,328 ha. Re-burned: 678,401 ha twice, 101,887 ha three times, 9,114 ha four times, 207 ha five times — [Mongabay 2020](https://mongabay.co.id/2020/10/30/greenpeace-nilai-omnibus-law-lemahkan-penegakan-hukum-kebakaran-hutan-dan-lahan/)
- Walhi "Siapa di Balik Api?", 1 Jan-7 Sep 2026: 9,951 hotspots in 561 oil palm HGU areas and 293 PBPH — [Katadata 16 Oct 2026](https://katadata.co.id/ekonomi-hijau/ekonomi-sirkular/6ad1b4ba9c1b5/gapki-dukung-larangan-warga-bakar-lahan-data-walhi-catat-titik-panas-di-konsesi). About 50% of hotspots Jan-Sep were in forestry and palm company areas — [Mongabay 24 Oct 2026](https://mongabay.co.id/2026/10/24/gelagapan-tangani-karhutla-pemerintah-malah-kambinghitamkan-tradisi-masyarakat-adat/). Walhi counts 372 companies with burn indications — [Katadata 28 Sep 2026](https://katadata.co.id/ekonomi-hijau/investasi-hijau/6aba5f1e16f88/kenapa-rencana-prabowo-cabut-8-perda-bakar-lahan-dipersoalkan-pegiat-lingkungan)
- Counter-evidence on attribution: CIFOR mapping of the 2014 Riau fires found some fires started outside concessions or on land held by small operators inside them, so hotspot-on-concession overlays can misattribute blame [SNIPPET] — [CIFOR Forests News](https://forestsnews.cifor.org/23479/new-maps-reveal-more-complex-picture-of-sumatran-fires?fnl=)
- A dissertation chapter concluded smallholder farms near settlements are the primary ignition sources in its study region and rejected the claim that most fires originate in oil palm concessions [SNIPPET, WEAK: author and region unverified] — [core.ac.uk](https://core.ac.uk/download/161454885.pdf)
- Sloan et al. 2022 (*Commun. Earth Environ.*): severe fire events in Indonesia fell 19-27% and very severe 23-34% over 2002-2019 after controlling for precipitation; declines concentrated in mosaic croplands and nearby forest, attributed to coordinated management of mosaic lands [SNIPPET] — [DOAJ record](https://doaj.org/article/95586aee87c34c80bb9d9e93240ff0da)
- Post-fire use, Pantau Gambut 2024: among burned peat in government restoration samples, 3% in state forest returned to forest; 57% of protected-function peat became oil palm. In concession samples 1% returned to forest, 48% monoculture, 50% scrub — [Mongabay 2024](https://mongabay.co.id/2024/08/13/kajian-sebut-gambut-bekas-terbakar-banyak-jadi-kebun-sawit/)

### Inferences
- Burned area inside concessions was roughly 29-36% of the 2015-2019 total (1,300,852 ha over 4.44 or 3.65 Mha, my calc; the numerator may be cumulative or unique, so treat as a range). This is location, not proof of who lit the fire.
- The "smallholder vs company" framing is partly false: Purnomo's network shows organisers and financiers sitting between the two, and land value rising after burning. Speculation is a price signal, not just a behaviour.
- Attribution is itself an information gap. Both sides of the 2026 argument use hotspot overlays, and CIFOR's critique applies to both.

### Gaps
- No share for accidental fire or arson found. BRIN-specific attribution data not found.
- Dennis et al. 2005 and Cattau's origin analysis were not retrieved (the Cattau hit was her RSPO paper).
- A "smallholders 1.6 Mha" figure from Chain Reaction Research appeared in a search summary only; not used. A KLHK "90% human" 2023 statement could not be tied to a URL; not used.

## 2. Peatland mechanics, rewetting infrastructure, BRGM

### Takeaway
40 cm is a defensible regulatory benchmark, but rewetting infrastructure is mostly temporary, unevenly maintained, and since 31 Dec 2024 has no clearly named owner. Field evidence is thin, and no BPK finding on sekat kanal was found.

### Cited Findings
- Pasal 23(3)(a) PP 57/2016: cultivation-function peat is damaged if groundwater is more than 0.4 m below the surface at a titik penaatan; measurement is at fixed points. The 0.4 m threshold already existed in PP 71/2014 [SNIPPET, official text not opened] — [Mongabay catatan hukum 2016](https://mongabay.co.id/2016/12/15/catatan-hukum-peraturan-gambut-lama-vs-baru/)
- Criticism: an IPB academic called the 0.4 m limit irrelevant as a damage rule; industry called constant compliance impossible [SNIPPET] — [Kontan](https://industri.kontan.co.id/news/kebijakan-perlindungan-gambut-ditentang-akademisi)
- Taufik et al. 2022 (8 stations, 2018-2019): high fire-danger class mostly appeared when water table fell below 40 cm; burned areas occurred only below 60 cm [SNIPPET] — [ASEAN Haze Portal](https://hazeportal.asean.org/?p=12604)
- 2018 proceedings (OKI, Kapuas) recommend 40 cm as threshold [SNIPPET] — [Unsri](https://conference.unsri.ac.id/index.php/semnashas/article/view/792)
- Page & Hooijer 2016 (note: 2016, not 2022) review links drainage to fire-driven carbon emissions; year-round peat mineralisation emissions are of similar magnitude to fire peaks [SNIPPET] — [Leicester figshare](https://figshare.le.ac.uk/articles/journal_contribution/In_the_line_of_fire_the_peatlands_of_Southeast_Asia/10147670)
- Canal block efficacy, Urzainki et al. 2023 (*Biogeosciences*; 168 blocks modelled, 22,000 ha, Sumatra): mean annual water table raised only 1.5 cm across scenarios; one-year effect reached about 600 m from canals; lowered water table in some places; "little evidence" of effectiveness at large scale [SNIPPET] — [Biogeosciences](https://bg.copernicus.org/articles/20/2099/2023/bg-20-2099-2023.html)
- Imanudin et al. 2021: blocking raised water levels, but in a dry year the 40 cm target was not reached (70 cm at end Dec 2019) [SNIPPET] — [Tropical Peatlands](https://www.tropicalpeatlands.org/knowledge/publication/11463-23298)
- Pantau Gambut "Gelisah di Lahan Basah" (7 provinces; 289 government and 240 concession points; Dec 2023-Mar 2024, rainy season): of 77 sampled government sekat kanal, 70% in worrying state from poor maintenance; 92% built of temporary wood/plank/sacking, 5% concrete or steel; 52% with TMAT beyond 40 cm. Many sumur bor poorly maintained, some blocked by soil. TMAT beyond 40 cm at 60% of government and 38% of concession samples. One point at PT Bumi Mekar Hijau reached 120 cm. Canals present at 54% of government samples and 49% of concession samples — [Mongabay 2024](https://mongabay.co.id/2024/08/13/kajian-sebut-gambut-bekas-terbakar-banyak-jadi-kebun-sawit/)
- Karya Indah, Kampar: 22 sekat kanal built by BRGM in 2019, 9 still effective in the 2024 evaluation; damage from a toll road, trucks, and residents [SNIPPET] — [Mongabay 2024](https://mongabay.co.id/2024/10/26/nasib-sekat-kanal-gambut-di-desa-karya-indah/)
- BRGM output: 1.6 Mha peat restored, 84,396 mangrove, 22,349 Infrastruktur Restorasi Gambut units combined (sumur bor, sekat kanal, kanal timbun). BRGM ended Dec 2024, in liquidation, with assets and obligations moved to related ministries; the article does not say who maintains the sites — [Kompas 21 Mar 2025](https://lestari.kompas.com/read/2025/03/21/095556186/brgm-restorasi-16-juta-hektare-gambut-dan-84396-hektare-mangrove?page=all)
- Formal end 31 Dec 2024 under Perpres 120/2020, dissolution letter 17 Apr 2025, tasks shared across ministries [SNIPPET; PDF unreadable] — [DPR Info Singkat Jun 2025](https://berkas.dpr.go.id/pusaka/files/info_singkat/Info%20Singkat-XVII-11-I-P3DI-Juni-2025-189.pdf)
- Madani: authority over restoration unclear after BRGM (KLH or Kemenhut named as possibilities); 26,761 ha of peat burned Jul-Aug 2025 — [Kompas 21 Oct 2025](https://lestari.kompas.com/read/2025/10/21/194000786/usai-brgm-dibubarkan-26.000-hektar-gambut-terbakar-siapa-kini-yang)
- The Forestry Minister publicly questioned the validity of TMAT data in Riau and Kalbar in July 2026 — [Metro TV 14 Jul 2026](https://www.metrotvnews.com/read/kewCjYAD-kemenhut-evaluasi-standar-personel-dan-alat-manggala-agni)
- Company TMAT compliance points exist as a data layer with company name, permit, point code in South Sumatra's geoportal, tagged SiMATAG-0,4m [SNIPPET] — [Data Gambut Sumsel](https://datagambut.sumselprov.go.id/layers/geonode:tp_tmat_perkebunan)

### Inferences
- Pantau Gambut sampled in the rainy season, so dry-season TMAT compliance is plausibly worse (not measured).
- Sekat kanal monitoring is cheap relative to fire response but matters only if someone owns repair. A "status board" without an owner reproduces the BRGM gap.
- Urzainki cuts against a "just build or fix blocks" pitch: at landscape scale, block condition may matter less than blocking density, peat conductivity and canal context.

### Gaps
- **No BPK report on sekat kanal, sumur bor or karhutla performance found** after three targeted searches. Check IHPS 2019-2025 directly. Adjacent only: BPK's 2021 DTT audit found control of unlicensed forest use non-compliant [SNIPPET] — [BPK](https://www.bpk.go.id/assets/files/attachments/attach_post_1648529442.pdf)
- BRGM budget for any year: not found. No separate national count of sekat kanal vs sumur bor. Official IRG functionality rate: not found. BRGM's area claim differs across reports (1.8 Mha in [Kompas 2024](https://lestari.kompas.com/read/2024/06/14/200000486/brgm-klaim-telah-restorasi-gambut-1-8-juta-hektar-sepanjang-2016-2023) vs 1.6 Mha) [headline only].
- Public access to company TMAT readings (vs point locations) unverified.

## 3. Legal ambiguity and alternatives to burning

### Takeaway
The ambiguity is now live and moving: Inpres 11/2026 adds a recommendation step, and the government intends to remove the exception by revising UU PPLH. Zero burning costs smallholders more than burning; hard Rp/ha figures are old and sparse.

### Cited Findings
- Pasal 69(1)(h) bans land clearing by burning; 69(2) gives regard to local wisdom. Penjelasan: max 2 ha per family head, local varieties, firebreak. Cipta Kerja (Pasal 22 angka 24 Perppu, ratified as UU 6/2023) restated both; the exception is not a basis for commercial clearing. Penalty per Hukumonline: up to 10 years and/or up to Rp2 billion (Pasal 108 as amended by UU 1/2026, with Pasal 79 UU 1/2023) — [Hukumonline Klinik, Jun 2026](https://www.hukumonline.com/klinik/a/hukumnya-membakar-hutan-untuk-membuka-lahan-lt56a70dd6773cd/)
- Permen LH 10/2010: max 2 ha per KK, local varieties, duty to notify kepala desa [SNIPPET; PDF blocked; procedure and current validity unverified] — [PDF copy](https://toolsfortransformation.net/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/PERMENLH-10_2010-Pencegahan-pencemaran-kerusahan-lingkungan.pdf)
- UU Cipta Kerja removed "without proof of fault" from Pasal 88, and qualified permit-holder fire responsibility in Pasal 49 UU Kehutanan with "berusaha bertanggungjawab"; Greenpeace says this weakens strict liability — [Mongabay 2020](https://mongabay.co.id/2020/10/30/greenpeace-nilai-omnibus-law-lemahkan-penegakan-hukum-kebakaran-hutan-dan-lahan/). GAPKI still raised Pasal 88 strict liability as a concern in Oct 2026 — [Katadata](https://katadata.co.id/ekonomi-hijau/ekonomi-sirkular/6ad1b4ba9c1b5/gapki-dukung-larangan-warga-bakar-lahan-data-walhi-catat-titik-panas-di-konsesi)
- Inpres 11/2026: local-wisdom burning allowed only with a central or regional government recommendation plus regional-government assistance. Minister Jumhur (13 Oct 2026) called the exemption a loophole, signalled removing it from UU PPLH (not yet revised), and proposed climate financing for non-burn clearing — [Katadata](https://katadata.co.id/ekonomi-hijau/investasi-hijau/6acdcf06526a5/menteri-jumhur-isyaratkan-hapus-pengecualian-bakar-lahan-oleh-masyarakat-adat)
- President Prabowo (24 Sep 2026) plans to revoke eight kabupaten perda allowing burning up to 2 ha; names not given. Walhi says this conflicts with Pasal 69 — [Katadata](https://katadata.co.id/ekonomi-hijau/investasi-hijau/6aba5f1e16f88/kenapa-rencana-prabowo-cabut-8-perda-bakar-lahan-dipersoalkan-pegiat-lingkungan)
- East Barito, Sep 2026: two Dayak Maanyan farmers detained. Police say nearly 11 ha; family says about 1 ha; Walhi says at most 2 ha and non-peat. Charged articles not named in the report; the case is ongoing — [Mongabay 11 Oct 2026](https://mongabay.co.id/2026/10/11/jerat-hukum-kasus-karhutla-pada-2-peladang-dayak-maanyan-tuai-kritik/)
- Cost: in Siak (2016; 40 farmers, 32 officials) actual zero-burning cost is more than twice controlled burning; the abstract gives no Rp/ha — [Biodiversitas 2018](https://smujo.id/biodiv/article/view/2799)
- Rp 5-7 million/ha minimum for non-burn agroforestry on peat, Central Kalimantan [WEAK: one farmer, 2016] — [Jakarta Post](https://www.thejakartapost.com/news/2016/10/06/fire-prevention-cultivating-land-without-burning)
- Nine Riau villages: zero burning takes longer, costs more labour, and often needs about 2 t/ha dolomite to replace ash; some owners abandoned farms [SNIPPET] — [The Conversation 2017](https://theconversation.com/zero-burning-policy-hurts-small-farmers-a-flexible-approach-is-needed-77969)
- Conflict: a 2002 IUCN/WWF review found zero burning not more expensive, likely reflecting larger commercial operations [SNIPPET] — [IUCN](https://iucn.org/node/11300)

### Inferences
- The 11 ha vs 1 ha dispute is a measurement and evidence failure that a geotagged plot record could narrow. It would not resolve the politics of who gets prosecuted.
- Inpres 11/2026 creates a new administrative workflow (request, recommendation, assistance) with no public procedure found. Any tool here depends on rules that may disappear if Pasal 69(2) is revised, so it carries high policy risk.
- Software cannot close a >2x cost gap. It can only route subsidy or carbon money if such money exists; the climate financing idea is so far a ministerial statement.

### Gaps
- Current Rp/ha for manual, mechanical, and herbicide clearing not found. Text of Inpres 11/2026 not opened (reported via press). Whether Permen LH 10/2010 remains in force unverified. ICEL's own analysis not retrieved. No statistic comparing individuals vs companies criminalised over time.

## 4. Enforcement and liability data

### Takeaway
Court wins do not convert to cash or restoration, and the state's 2026 enforcement counts are inconsistent and largely anonymised. This is the most clearly information-shaped failure, although the core barrier is judicial and political will.

### Cited Findings
- As of Nov 2023: 22 companies sued since 2013, 14 liable with appeals exhausted, Rp5.6 trillion ordered. Only PT BMH (Rp78.5 bn) paid in full; none of the 14 had restored burnt concessions. Barriers: repeat legal challenges, weak institutional support for execution, banks requiring an OJK letter before disclosing data, installments the rulings did not allow — [Mongabay via APSN](https://www.asia-pacific-solidarity.net/news/2023-11-07/burn-now-pay-later-fines-trickle-indonesias-crackdown-forest-fires.html)
- Execution sits with the Ketua PN. Reported: chiefs not acting on submitted asset data, refusing a bailiff for appraisal, suspending execution because of a PK. Unclear whether restoration means money or work. Walhi Riau's count of unexecuted corporate civil judgments was about Rp17.761 trillion (own figure, Dec 2020) — [Hukumonline 2020](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/beberapa-tantangan-dalam-eksekusi-putusan-karhutla-lt5fc7387cd884d/?page=all)
- Greenpeace/ministry case summaries in 2019: unpaid fire and logging penalties over $220m, about $1.3bn including a 2013 logging case [SNIPPET] — [Al Jazeera 2019](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2019/2/15/indonesia-land-burning-fines-unpaid-years-after-disastrous-fires)
- Kallista Alam: Rp366 bn final since 2015. Reports conflict on payment: Rp57 bn by Sep 2023 (APSN above) vs Rp114 bn material damages paid in 2023; Rp251 bn restoration on 1,000 ha not carried out [WEAK: opinion] — [Katadata 11 Sep 2026](https://katadata.co.id/amp/indepth/opini/6aa173522d332/rente-kedaruratan-di-balik-asap-dan-abu)
- Repeat burning and sanctions: only 2 of the 10 largest oil palm burners got administrative sanctions per Greenpeace; PT BMH burned 87,600 ha and PT BAP 84,400 ha in 2015-2019 (companies have disputed Greenpeace's data) — [Mongabay 2020](https://mongabay.co.id/2020/10/30/greenpeace-nilai-omnibus-law-lemahkan-penegakan-hukum-kebakaran-hutan-dan-lahan/)
- 2026, Kemenhut: 32 companies sanctioned (19 Oct). Five permits revoked and named in full; 19 suspended + forced recovery (about 9,500 ha); 8 forced recovery (about 2,200 ha). The rest are listed by initials only — [Katadata 19 Oct 2026](https://katadata.co.id/ekonomi-hijau/investasi-hijau/6ad5ea6bd3203/32-perusahaan-kena-sanksi-kebakaran-hutan-termasuk-pemasok-asia-pulp-and-paper)
- 2026, Polri: 11 corporate suspects, names not released, 3 foreign-invested; Greenpeace demands disclosure. Singapore's NEA publicly named 10 companies — [Katadata 22 Oct 2026](https://katadata.co.id/ekonomi-hijau/investasi-hijau/6ad9a4a059faa/greenpeace-desak-pemerintah-buka-identitas-11-korporasi-tersangka-karhutla)
- Other 2026 counts: Bareskrim 162 suspects and 33 corporations under investigation ([Katadata 28 Sep](https://katadata.co.id/ekonomi-hijau/investasi-hijau/6aba5f1e16f88/kenapa-rencana-prabowo-cabut-8-perda-bakar-lahan-dipersoalkan-pegiat-lingkungan)); 138 individuals, 68 concession companies sealed by KLH ([Mongabay 24 Oct](https://mongabay.co.id/2026/10/24/gelagapan-tangani-karhutla-pemerintah-malah-kambinghitamkan-tradisi-masyarakat-adat/)); 125 legal subjects in criminal process ([InfoPublik 21 Oct](https://infopublik.id/kategori/bencana/994025/kemenhut-tegaskan-penanganan-karhutla-terus-berjalan-32-perusahaan-dijatuhi-sanksi))
- Concession overlay and public data: Walhi could rank companies but press did not name the top ones — [Katadata 16 Oct](https://katadata.co.id/ekonomi-hijau/ekonomi-sirkular/6ad1b4ba9c1b5/gapki-dukung-larangan-warga-bakar-lahan-data-walhi-catat-titik-panas-di-konsesi)

### Inferences
- Published "unpaid" totals are not comparable. Rp5.6T (14 fire cases) vs Rp17-19T ranges likely reflects inclusion of the Rp16T PT Merbau Pelalawan Lestari illegal-logging case ([Eco-Business, SNIPPET](https://www.eco-business.com/news/indonesian-logging-firm-fined-a-record-us12-billion-for-deforestation)). Verify before quoting any headline number.
- The differing 2026 counts have different dates and agencies, so they may not contradict. There is, however, no single public ledger reconciling them, and that is the point.
- A public tracker from court decisions (putusan.mahkamahagung.go.id) and press releases is feasible for a student team and hits a real gap. It would create pressure, not execution; asset discovery remains behind OJK and court gatekeeping.

### Gaps
- No dated official total of paid vs owed in 2025-2026. Auriga's own compiled dataset not found (only quoted comment). Other press totals (Rp17.82T, about Rp18T, Rp19.3T) seen only in search listings, e.g. [Pulitzer Center](https://pulitzercenter.org/id/node/19111), [Tirto](https://tirto.id/dgZ6). Concession-overlap statistics (permit vs permit, permit vs customary) not found. Public availability of full HGU maps not checked here.

## 5. Institutions, staffing, coordination, budget

### Takeaway
Numbers are fragmentary, usually operation-specific. National Manggala Agni headcount, Desa Mandiri Peduli Api coverage, and per-province budgets were not found. Reported budget friction is about disbursement and allocation size more than a documented karhutla-line cut.

## 7. Single points of failure and the causal map

### Takeaway
The best-evidenced information/coordination failures are in the handoffs after detection: who acts on an alert, who owns a built asset, who tracks a ruling or a disbursement. Satellite detection is not the bottleneck. **I found no BPK report supporting these; the strongest "official" text is an Ombudsman representative's op-ed, which explicitly does not conclude maladministration.**

### Cited Findings
- Ombudsman Babel, 31 Aug 2026: risk is mappable, hotspots detectable; the danger is data that "berhenti di layar pemantauan". Late information, agencies waiting on each other, and unanswered public reports each break the chain; needs detection, follow-up, prevention, oversight, evaluation, with a named responsible party — [Ombudsman](https://ombudsman.go.id/news/download/pwkmedia--jangan-menunggu-api-menguji-tata-kelola-negara)
- Ombudsman RI is preparing a special investigation into maladministration in fire response [SNIPPET; date not shown] — [Ombudsman](https://ombudsman.go.id/perwakilan/news/r/pwkmedia--ombudsman-ri-siapkan-investigasi-khusus-karhutla-luas-kebakaran-di-sulsel-1489-hektare)
- Rp290.9 bn still awaited in DPR's Aug 2026 hearing [SNIPPET] — [Antara](https://jogja.antaranews.com/berita/849456/kemenkeu-didesak-cairkan-anggaran-darurat-rp290-miliar-untuk-karhutla)
- Burned area context: 107,465 ha Jan-Jun 2026 per Kemenhut [SNIPPET] — [RCTI+](https://www.rctiplus.com/news/detail/terkini/5431931/karhutla-di-indonesia-capai-107-465-hektare--kalbar-paling-luas-terdampak)

### Inferences

Causal chain: **land value + weak tenure → organised clearing (burn is cheapest) → ignition on drained peat in drought → late or unowned response → large fire → air-heavy emergency spending → weak or unexecuted liability → burned land still gains value → repeat.**
